# Task 3: Sales Performance Dashboard

**DataX Labs — Data Analyst Internship (Ministry of MSME, Govt. of India)**

## Objective
Design an interactive dashboard for business stakeholders to explore sales performance, revenue trends, and product/regional breakdowns.

## Tools Used
- Tableau Public (free desktop tool)

## Dataset
Sales_Financial dataset (Kaggle)

## What's in the Dashboard
- **KPI Cards** — Total Revenue, Total Orders, Total Units Sold, Average Order Value
- **Revenue Over Time** — monthly line chart showing revenue trend across the year
- **Revenue by Product Type** — horizontal bar chart ranking product categories by revenue
- **Revenue by State** — choropleth map with revenue shown on a color gradient
- **Filters/Slicers** — Product Type, Year of Order Date, State, Revenue range, and Product
- **Consistent color theme** applied across all charts and KPI cards
- **Dashboard title** — "2019 Sales Performance Overview"

## Key Insights
- Laptops are the top-performing product type by revenue, followed by Monitors and iPhones.
- California is the leading state by revenue, likely reflecting its large tech-buyer base.
- Revenue shows seasonal dips mid-year with a strong recovery toward Q4 (November–December).
